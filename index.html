Our Story

Made by Hand.
Made With Love.

At Cozy Petals, we believe flowers should preserve memories forever, not just for a few days.

Each individual petal, bud, and stem is shaped with precision using plush chenille pipe-cleaners, artistic wrapping, and endless patience by Tanu in Khunti, Jharkhand. Turning soft craft wire into everlasting keepsakes that bring warmth to every desk, bedroom, and celebration.
